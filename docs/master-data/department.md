# Department Management Architecture & Specification

## 1. Executive Overview

In **SHEHSWAR ONE360**, a `Department` represents a functional division, cost center, or operational department within a legal entity. Departments organize staff members, govern internal approval workflows, and establish cost attribution.

### Core Standard Departments
- **Production & Formulation** (Chemical reactors, synthesis, packaging)
- **Quality Control & Assurance (QC/QA)** (Laboratory testing, COA verification)
- **Procurement & Supply Chain** (Raw materials, packaging, vendor relations)
- **Finance & Treasury** (Accounts, billing, ledger, audit)
- **Commercial Sales & Distribution** (Dealer accounts, order dispatch)
- **Warehousing & Logistics** (Material movement, inventory custody)
- **Human Resources & Administration** (Talent management, compliance)

---

## 2. Entity Schema & Standards

| Field | Type | Constraint | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `id` | `VARCHAR(36)` | `PRIMARY KEY` | UUIDv7 primary identifier |
| `organizationId` | `VARCHAR(36)` | `NOT NULL, FK` | Enterprise holding scope |
| `companyId` | `VARCHAR(36)` | `NOT NULL, FK` | Operating legal entity |
| `code` | `VARCHAR(50)` | `NOT NULL` | Functional code (e.g. `DEP-PROD`, `DEP-QC`) |
| `name` | `VARCHAR(255)` | `NOT NULL` | Full department title |
| `managerName` | `VARCHAR(255)` | `NULLABLE` | Head of Department (HOD) / General Manager |
| `costCenter` | `VARCHAR(100)` | `NULLABLE` | General ledger cost center reference (e.g. `CC-4010`) |
| `isActive` | `BOOLEAN` | `NOT NULL, DEFAULT true` | Active operational status |
| `createdAt` | `TIMESTAMPTZ` | `NOT NULL` | Creation timestamp |
| `updatedAt` | `TIMESTAMPTZ` | `NOT NULL` | Modification timestamp |

---

## 3. Workflow Integration

Departments are central to the **Approval Foundation** in SHEHSWAR ONE360:
- Purchase requisitions generated by the Production department automatically route through the departmental HOD before advancing to Procurement.
- Quality test holds on batches require sign-off by designated QC departmental personnel.

---

## 4. UI Architecture & Features

- **Department List**: Filterable by company and status, with employee count aggregates and cost center identifiers.
- **Department Detail**: Overview of assigned leadership, operational staff roster, linked cost centers, and change history.
- **Department Form**: Standard `FormPageTemplate` with code uniqueness checks and validation.
